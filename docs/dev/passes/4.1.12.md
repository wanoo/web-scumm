## Not done, said as such

The Studio's demo mode shows the IR without provenance and cannot write objectives (the dev server can); no Reality
nor stage primitive admitted (each refused with its proof in DSL-STABILITY); `e2e:canonical` written in this lot and
run by CI only where its row is wired (the LOG says which); the mutation of the new core files (read on the next
nightly).
