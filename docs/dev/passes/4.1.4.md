# Passes, 4.1.4 "Honest Engine"

The plan's third release: what the engine must hold when things end or fail, each answered at the tag; then the
passes only people can make (D12).

| # | It may not ship while… | At the tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a script that throws stays "running" in the save and dies in silence | stopped, `scripts[id].off`, said through `onError` (`tests/engine-honesty.test.ts`) |
| 2 | a destroyed engine or player keeps a loop, a listener, a frame or a blob URL | `Engine.destroy`, `App.destroy`; built and destroyed twenty times with no listener left (`tests/dom/app-destroy.test.ts`) |
| 3 | the time in a state, a trace or a session comes from anything but the clock | two new games under one clock are identical (`tests/engine-honesty.test.ts`, `tests/critical-session.test.ts`) |
| 4 | the player reaches its goals by replacing the engine's methods | `beforeSave` and `onLoad`, tested; `app.ts` patches nothing |
| 5 | a key of the state is spelled in more than one place | `core/keys.ts`, the ten sites through it, a 4.0 save byte-identical (`tests/keys.test.ts`, the golden saves) |
| 6 | a dead file or dependency can stay unnoticed | `knip` in `npm run quality` |
