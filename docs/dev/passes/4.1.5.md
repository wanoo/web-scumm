# Passes, 4.1.5 "Real Core"

The plan's fourth release: the split of 4.1.0 made real, each step answered at the tag; then the passes only people
can make (D12).

| # | It may not ship while… | At the tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `step()` is one 448-line function | a table of handlers, one per command key, type-checked against the union (`core/command-handlers.ts`) |
| 2 | the script loops, the session, the camera and the walking are fields on the engine or the view that functions mutate | `ScriptScheduler`, `SessionLog` (`engine.sessions`), `Camera` (`view.camera`), `Walker` (`view.walker`), each with its tests (`tests/engine-honesty.test.ts`, `tests/critical-session.test.ts`, `tests/dom/camera-walker.test.ts`) |
| 3 | the keyboard targets and the inventory are thrown away and made again on every state change | diffed by id; a focused target keeps its focus (`tests/dom/render-diff.test.ts`) |
| 4 | `dom/room.ts` needs an exception to the 800-line limit | 744 lines, off the list (`tests/file-size.test.ts`) |
| 5 | a step changes behaviour | the quality baseline identical after each branch, the visual baselines 3/3, the frame rate 3/3 rooms at 30 fps or more at ÷4 |
| 6 | the step table and the scheduler are outside the mutation set | in the core set (`tools/mutate.ts`), measured in MUTANTS.md |

## Not done, said as such

`busyCount`, `guideWait` and `skipping` stay on the Engine (no `Busy` owner); the player (`dom/app.ts`, 795 lines,
42 `@internal` fields) is not decomposed into `InputState`, `SpeechController`, `CallFrame`, `MapView`; the Canvas
painter still builds its draw list per frame; the hit boxes are not precomputed; the guests are computed once per
`targets()`, not memoised by state version. 4.1.6 and 4.1.7 do not depend on them.
