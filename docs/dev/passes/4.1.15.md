# Passes, 4.1.15 "Remix"

The programme's eighth and last release (§11 of `docs/dev/PLAN-4.1.8-4.1.15.md`, `docs/dev/plans/4.1.15-remix.md`),
each exit criterion answered at the tag; then the passes only people can make (D12: reported, not blocking in 4.1.x;
blocking for 4.2.0 on every surface announced as supported, D18). Remix is `experimental` until five human seeds.

| # | It may not ship while… | At the tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the same seed gives two worlds on two runtimes | `tests/remix-compile.test.ts` (determinism, `canonicalJson` before every hash, `sha256` against WebCrypto on edge inputs); `e2e:remix` on Chromium, WebKit and Firefox **written, not run** (said as such) |
| 2 | a valid seed can fail to yield a world | catalogues are total and uniform; the generator backtracks (a test on the dead-end example); a malformed seed or unknown version is an explicit error |
| 3 | an old save loses its world or is overwritten | `tests/remix-saves.test.ts`: v3 → story world, every golden save 3.0.0 → 4.1.14 loads; a save of another world kept, the title asks (5 DOM tests) |
| 4 | a world is not proved | `npm run verify:variants` inside `verify:game`: every world of every catalogue mode solved with a certificate (demo 1 + 3, reference 1 + 24 + 24 + 24) |
| 5 | `Math.random` reaches the logical path | the Biome rule (aliased `Math` included) over `src/engine/core` and the minigames, two tests (grep and a throwing `Math.random`); minigames draw from the seeded stream |
| 6 | the Bridge can choose a seed after seeing actions | `tests/remix-daily.test.ts`: commit before, reveal after, offline check with the published key; the leftover (seed shopping within 3 commits/hour, start within 60 s of the reveal) in `docs/dev/threat-models/remix-seed.md` |
| 7 | the wheel has no solution or no accessible alternative | the validator checks a solution exists per wheel; buttons, keys, gamepad and a text list; no animation under reduced motion (`tests/code-wheel.test.ts`); `e2e:a11y` over the wheel **not run** |

## Not done, said as such

`e2e:remix` and `e2e:a11y` over the wheel (written, not run here, not in CI); the five human playtest seeds; the daily
module not mounted on `bridge/src/server.ts` nor on a `RealityStore`; the `.wsrun` envelope carries no `variant`
(the verifier must be handed `applyVariant`); a wheel's record is a DOM event, not in the session; the wheel's `story`
mode ends like `parody`; Mystery seed shopping limited, not prevented.
