The rehearsal of 4.2 (`docs/dev/PLAN-4.1.18-DRESS-REHEARSAL.md`, D32): the tools that make the people's passes
reproducible (the Field Kit), the two remaining mutation sets gated, the archives consumed outside the repository on
three systems, and the Bridge's qualification profile. Each automatic criterion of the plan's §18 answered at the tag,
then the people's passes (D12: reported, not blocking in 4.1.x; D18: no surface promoted without its pass).

## Known limits, said as such

- **No people's pass was made for 4.1.18**: thirteen open `field-pass` issues (#75 to #87). The release is a
  rehearsal; 4.2 cannot start before them (GO / NO-GO: NO-GO today).
- **The tag is not signed** and nothing is published to npm: both 4.2's (D30, D18).
- The qualification profile does not yet run the speedrun queue and its workers, Daily and Mystery, quotas,
  moderation or a 4.1.17 store's migration inside its topology: each is judged by its own job (`ops/qualify/README.md`).
- The `distributed` profile and the four connectors stay **experimental** until their passes (#77, #79).
- The leaderboard is per pseudonym (no player identity); a printed code wheel is `valid-unranked` until a moderator
  ranks it; the live RTA after a resume may count before the run's start (sealed times right): `livesplit-obs` (#81)
  is where it gets a test and a fix if it is still there.
